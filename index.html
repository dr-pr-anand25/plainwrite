Questions

What is an AI humanizer?

A tool that rewrites machine-sounding text so it reads like a person wrote it: fewer stock phrases, more varied sentences, plainer words.

How do I make AI text sound human?

Paste it into the box, choose a style, press Humanize it. Then read the result aloud, click any sentence that still sounds off, and rephrase it.

Is it free for everyone?

Yes. There is no limit on reruns, no credits and no payment screen.

Will it keep my meaning?

It swaps wording and reshapes sentences, and it does not add claims. Lock names, numbers and keywords in the "Keep as written" field, and always read the result before you publish it.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page and paste. With Fast rules your text stays in your browser.

Will it get past AI detectors?

No tool can promise that, and detectors disagree with each other. The "stock phrases flagged" count under the output shows a concrete thing you can check: how many telltale phrases the rewrite removed.
